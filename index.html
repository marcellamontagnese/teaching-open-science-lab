About

These tools were developed for teaching research methods and scientific integrity at the University of Cambridge. They are designed to complement lectures on reproducibility, open science, and the replication crisis.

All activities run entirely in the browser — no installation, accounts, or server required. Students can access them on any device by visiting this page.
